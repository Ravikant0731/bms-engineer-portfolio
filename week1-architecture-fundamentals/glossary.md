# BMS/BAS Glossary — Week 1

**BMS (Building Management System):** A centralized system that monitors and controls a building's mechanical and electrical equipment, primarily HVAC, with interfaces to lighting, energy metering, and fire/access systems.

**BAS (Building Automation System):** Functionally identical to BMS; terminology varies by region (BAS common in the US, BMS common in India/Middle East).

**AHU (Air Handling Unit):** A unit that mixes fresh and return air, filters it, conditions it (cooling/heating), and supplies it through ductwork to a building zone.

**FCU (Fan Coil Unit):** A smaller, zone-level unit with a coil and fan that recirculates and conditions room air, without its own fresh-air intake.

**Chiller:** Equipment that produces chilled water (typically 6-7°C supply, 12-13°C return) used to cool air in AHU/FCU cooling coils.

**VFD (Variable Frequency Drive):** A device that controls motor speed by varying the frequency of AC power, used for energy-efficient fan/pump control.

**VAV (Variable Air Volume box):** A zone-level device with a modulating damper that controls airflow into a specific zone based on temperature demand.

**DDC (Direct Digital Control):** A microprocessor-based controller that reads sensor inputs, runs control logic (often PID), and drives actuator outputs.
